Newgroove Studio · The Mission

Solving the Musician’s Problem

A home for the music that would otherwise never get made.

by Glenroy Bramble

Ask most musicians if they have a good recording of their own music, and watch their heads drop. So much talent — and so little to show for the countless hours spent honing it. Studio time is expensive, schedules never line up, and a phone propped on a table was never going to capture what you hear in your head.

Newgroove Studio exists to fix that. It’s a place to set up your instruments, sit down with other players, and create — live, with real drums, no clock running, no meter spinning. Play it back the moment you land it. Chase the riff while it’s still hot. Because when musicians are comfortable and inspired, that’s when the magic happens. Collaboration is the key.

But a great recording is only half the problem. Once you’ve made something you’re proud of, who’s going to hear it? That’s why Newgroove is more than a room — it’s a network built to get your music made, heard, and passed on.

What we’re building

  • The Room — jam and record live with immediate playback, backed by a network of musicians and writers. No pressure of time or money.
  • The Network — every musician gets a page (bio, photos, music, credits, searchable), so listeners discover you and each other, and anyone hiring can hear your sound first.
  • The Next Generation — lessons, instruments, and a nurturing place for young players to learn from experienced musicians and reach an audience.
  • The Audience — a public home for the music, a community that gives feedback and inspiration, and membership that keeps it running.

This is what I set out to build. Newgroove Studio — where the music finally gets made, heard, and passed on.
